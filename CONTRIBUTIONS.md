## 2. CRediT Roles Taxonomy

Contributors are mapped to the standard Contributor Roles Taxonomy (CRediT):
- **Conceptualization:** Formulating overarching research goals, hypotheses, and experimental questions.
- **Methodology:** Designing statistical protocols, measurement budget matching, and algebraic identities.
- **Software:** Implementing Hamiltonian models, quantum kernel engines, measurement noise simulators, and classical baselines.
- **Validation:** Independent verification, execution of integration test suites, and cross-reviewer checks.
- **Formal Analysis:** Deriving mathematical representations, degree weighting proofs, and statistical inference (Wilcoxon tests, Holm corrections).
- **Investigation:** Running numerical diagonalization, kernel Gram generation, and hyperparameter tuning sweeps.
- **Data Curation:** Managing benchmark records, schema validation, and artifact versioning.
- **Writing – Original Draft:** Preparation of initial manuscript sections, tables, and architectural specifications.
- **Writing – Review & Editing:** Critical review, commentary, and revision across cross-reviewer pairs.
- **Visualization:** Generating vector publication figures, learning curves, and shot scaling plots.
- **Supervision:** Research oversight and quality gate enforcement.
- **Project Administration:** Managing timelines, work packages, quality gates, and issue registries.

## 3. Authorship Rule
Authorship is governed strictly by substantive intellectual contribution, active drafting or critical revision of the manuscript, and final approval and accountability for the submitted work.
- Rigorous negative/null results and the discovery of central methodological flaws count fully as substantive intellectual contributions.
- Final authorship decisions are made at manuscript freeze based on verified contributions, not by prior promise or allocation.
